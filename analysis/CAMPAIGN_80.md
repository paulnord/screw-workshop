## Validation

- All80 authored physical routes:497 moves, actual off-board completion, legal placement, bounded hinges/contacts, no initial overlap. Maximum observed replay contact overlap2.44px.
- All48 new puzzles replay after Undo. All80 targets reach the original-screw-count lower bound under intended anchoring rules. This is not a proof against numerical constraint exploits.
- Historical294 alternate witness routes still pass.
- New reverse-order probes (one fixed reverse release order per puzzle, legal parking search, no reparking, settle2400steps, cap100nodes):23 solved,8 finite-model rejections,17 unresolved. These probes are not full solution counts or a human difficulty rating.
- Progress tests preserve old scores, stable-ID and numeric resumes, previously earned pack unlocks; new unlock threshold7/10. An independent65,536-profile legacy migration audit passed.
- Stars reward moves, not speed; Undo removes undone moves, hints are free, any completion earns a star.
- Four puzzles total have an extra covered parking target, all in Hidden in plain sight; each tested route uses its target.

Cloud-browser QA verified narrow320/390px layouts, the ten-puzzle selector, new-puzzle hints, Undo, three-move completion/stars and persistence. This is not an actual iPhone Safari device test.
